## About Me

I am a Systems Analysis and Development student based in **Brazil**, currently diving deep into
**backend development** and **information security**. Originally from the suburbs of Belo Horizonte,
I built my entire path in technology on my own — without mentors or formal support — through
persistence, self-teaching, and a genuine passion for how systems work underneath.

I have been using **Linux for over three years** and fully migrated from Windows about a year ago.
This repo is my profile — a snapshot of who I am, what I do, and where I am headed.
